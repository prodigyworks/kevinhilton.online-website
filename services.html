Services

At Kevin Hilton Online, we deliver a comprehensive suite of technology solutions designed to empower your business. From cutting-edge cloud-based web systems and bespoke mobile app development to seamless AI integrations and tailored IT services, our team ensures every solution is perfectly aligned with your unique needs. We don’t just provide technology—we craft intelligent, scalable systems that drive efficiency, enhance user experience, and give you a competitive edge in an ever-evolving digital landscape. With our expertise, your ideas are transformed into reliable, future-ready solutions.

Planning Board

Our Planning Board software provides a complete solution for capacity planning across machines and staff. It combines real-time data with advanced analytics to give you clear insights into resource utilization, helping you make informed decisions. With robust reporting tools, you can track performance, identify bottlenecks, and optimize productivity, ensuring your operations run smoothly and efficiently.

Audit

Our Audit software gives you full control over internal and external auditing processes. It allows you to create configurable audits tailored to your business needs, capture digital signatures, and upload images for detailed reporting. This solution simplifies compliance, enhances accountability, and ensures that all audit activities are documented securely and efficiently.

Transport Management

Our flagship Transport Management system is designed to revolutionize logistics operations. It offers intelligent route planning and optimization, booking management, and online proof of delivery (POD). Drivers benefit from a mobile app that handles vehicle checklists and POD uploads, while managers gain complete visibility and control over their fleet, maximizing efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Modelling Booking System

The Modelling Booking System streamlines the entire booking process from lead generation to confirmed appointment. Featuring a dynamic calendar of scheduled bookings, contract signing functionality, and a visual workflow that moves leads through each stage, it ensures every step is tracked and managed efficiently. This system keeps your team organized while providing a seamless experience for your clients.

Sports Booking System

Our Sports Booking System is tailored for leisure centres and sports facilities, making court and activity bookings simple and efficient. Clients can easily register, view availability, and book sessions online, while integrated payment gateways handle secure transactions seamlessly. The system streamlines scheduling, reduces administrative overhead, and provides real-time updates, ensuring both staff and clients enjoy a smooth, professional experience every time.

Task Management

Our Task Management system helps teams stay organized, productive, and on track. It allows you to create, assign, and track tasks with ease, set deadlines, and monitor progress in real time. With features like priority tagging, notifications, and collaborative tools, your team can work efficiently, ensure accountability, and meet deadlines consistently, all within a single, easy-to-use platform.
