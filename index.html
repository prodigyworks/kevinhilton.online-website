A web domain is the address of your website on the internet — what people type in their browser to find you (for example, www.yourbusiness.com). Think of it as your website’s street address.

Web hosting is the service that stores your website’s files and makes them accessible online. A hosting provider keeps your website running 24/7, ensuring visitors can load pages quickly and securely.

Together, the domain and hosting allow your website to be found and used by anyone, anywhere.

The choice between Linux and Windows servers depends on the technologies your website uses:

  • Linux servers are ideal for most websites, especially those built with PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, HTML, and WordPress. They are stable, secure, cost-effective, and widely used in the industry.
  • Windows servers are typically used if your website relies on .NET, ASP, or Microsoft SQL Server. They are also reliable and integrate well with other Microsoft services.

In general, Linux hosting is more common for web projects, but the right server always depends on your website’s specific requirements.

Both Google Cloud (GCP) and Amazon Web Services (AWS) are leading cloud platforms, offering reliable, high-performance hosting for websites and applications.

  • AWS is the most widely used and offers a huge range of services and global server locations. It’s highly flexible and scales easily for businesses of all sizes.
  • Google Cloud is known for fast network speeds, strong AI and data tools, and excellent integration with Google services. It’s particularly good for applications that rely on analytics or machine learning.

Which is fastest? Performance depends more on server location, configuration, and how your site is built, rather than the platform itself. Both AWS and Google Cloud can deliver excellent speed and reliability when properly set up.

Organic SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website appear naturally in search engine results, without paying for ads. It’s important because:

  • Increases visibility: More potential customers can find your business when searching online.
  • Builds credibility: Higher rankings signal trust and authority to users.
  • Drives long-term traffic: Unlike paid ads, good SEO continues to attract visitors over time.
  • Cost-effective growth: Investing in organic SEO gives ongoing returns compared to short-term ad campaigns.

In short, SEO ensures your business can be discovered online, attract the right audience, and grow sustainably.
